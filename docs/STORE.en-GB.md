**Agentenblick — understand AI traffic reaching your shop**

See which crawlers and agents visit your Shopware shop and what supports their claimed identities. Agentenblick records server-side observations. By default it adds no visitor JavaScript; an optional experimental WebMCP introduction uses a local script. It adds no cookie.

The native administration combines an overview, agent list, CSV export, observed journey steps and evaluated failures. Signature verification, official IP ranges, confirmed reverse DNS and declared identities remain visibly distinct. Missing steps are not invented and an order is not treated as proof of payment.

Health diagnostics compare saved robots rules with observed errors and report discovery endpoint statuses. Public probes require an explicit action. Optional user-agent tests originate from the shop server, so they do not prove how a real operator's traffic is handled.

The optional benchmark is disabled by default. Review the exact aggregate payload and explicitly consent before sending. Small local denominators are suppressed; published metrics require at least five eligible installations. Demonstration data are separate.

**Limits:** Traffic served entirely by a CDN is invisible. Trusted proxies must be configured correctly. Browser agents that resemble people may remain unidentified. Experimental automation detection is disabled by default; its detection and human false-positive targets have not been validated. Suspected automation does not establish AI use, operator identity or a customer's mandate.

Store acceptance is not claimed. See README and the acceptance reports for measured evidence.
